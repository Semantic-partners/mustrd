Two little ontologies for places & the people who govern them

The geography example measures term coverage across a pair of cross-referencing vocabularies. place describes geographic things and how they nest; governance adds a civic role that reuses both place and an external vocabulary (foaf).

place: 6 classes · 2 properties

A minimal vocabulary for places and how they are geographically contained within one another.

gov: 1 class · 1 property

http://example.org/governance#

A tiny vocabulary for governance roles and the places they govern.

  • externalfoaf:Person referenced, not declared here
    • classgov:Mayor A person who governs a city.
02

Properties

place:isLocatedIn
owl:ObjectProperty owl:TransitiveProperty
place:Place → place:Place

Relates a place to a place that geographically contains it. Transitive, so containment chains (a city in a country in a continent) close automatically.

gov:governs
owl:ObjectProperty
foaf:Person → place:City

Relates a person to a place they are responsible for governing — the cross-ontology link from governance into place.

place:basedOnStandard
owl:OntologyProperty
ontology-level metadata — no domain / range

The published standard this vocabulary is based on. Documentation metadata rather than a domain term, so coverage treats it as schema, not a gap.

place: namespace governance: namespace external (foaf:) — referenced, not declared → domain to range
